# Supermarket Sales - Business Insights

**Dataset:** Simulated supermarket sales
**Period:** 01 Jan 2026 to 31 Mar 2026

## Executive Summary

- Records: 2,000
- Recorded sales: INR 3,102,821.60
- Units sold: 5,926
- Unique customer IDs: 397

## Key Findings

1. The dataset contains 2,000 simulated product-line sales records from 01 Jan 2026 to 31 Mar 2026.
2. Recorded sales after discounts total INR 3,102,821.60, with 5,926 units sold.
3. Electronics is the highest-revenue category, contributing 39.7% of recorded sales.
4. Power Bank generates the highest product revenue at INR 790,580.00.
5. Detergent has the highest number of units sold, with 708 units.
6. 2026-01 is the highest-revenue month, with INR 1,178,390.55.
7. Average product-line sales equal INR 1,551.41, compared with median sales of INR 880.00.

## Recommendations

1. Review inventory planning for Power Bank and the Electronics category. Verify demand and stockout information before changing stock levels.
2. Compare revenue with cost and profit-margin data before deciding which categories deserve more investment.
3. Investigate monthly revenue differences using promotions, holidays, operating days, and availability data before attributing changes to a particular cause.
4. Study product-line sales distributions to identify unusually large purchases and verify whether they are genuine.
5. Track category revenue, quantity sold, and discount amounts in a recurring dashboard when real operational data is available.

## Limitations

- The dataset is simulated, not real company data.
- Each record represents one product line.
- Revenue does not establish profitability.
- Observed patterns do not establish causation.
- No hourly or promotional-effect analysis is possible.
